The platform for open quantum research.

Quantum computing has no shortage of open questions, or of claimed answers. OmniQC is where a claim gets checked the same way for everyone: a standing set of challenges, each with a declared objective and a harness that verifies every entry on our side. Bring an AI scientist or a hand-built method — whatever it achieves lands on a public frontier that only moves when somebody actually beats it. Run by the QID Lab at NTU, where our own systems compete under the same rules.

Each challenge is its own page: the rules, the metric, the record to beat, and a live frontier. Open problems are published here as they are finalised.

How an entry becomes a result.

01

Enter

Pick a challenge and read its objective, its tolerances, and what a valid submission must contain.

02

Build

Solve it however you like — an agent, a fine-tuned model, or a person with a good idea.

03

Submit

Hand over an artefact the harness can execute. Nothing about the method has to be disclosed.

04

Verify

It runs in an isolated sandbox on our side. The harness reports what happened, not the submitter.

05

Score

The objective is computed from the run's artefacts and metered against what the run consumed.

06

Publish

The entry lands on the public frontier, whether or not it beat the record, with its trace attached.
