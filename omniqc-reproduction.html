Manuscript · 2026

Reimagining computational quantum research with AI scientists

A new paradigm in which published results become reusable scientific capabilities for autonomous discovery.

Yuxuan Du, Hanqing Zhao, Xinbiao Wang, Yiming Lu, Kaining Zhang, Dacheng Tao

Background

AI scientists are expanding automated research across scientific domains, yet turning the scientific record into a foundation for autonomous discovery remains elusive. OmniQ, an expert-audited virtual research environment, converts published studies in classical simulation, quantum algorithms and quantum-property characterization into executable reproduction tasks.

Evaluated across five LLM-powered agentic systems and six third-party agent harnesses, current AI scientists often fail. Part of the gap lies in the record itself: in many studies the paper, the released code and the reported results disagree. Expert intervention turns such failures into successful reproductions, showing that the missing knowledge can be identified and reused.

Residual Agents (RA), a self-improving AI scientist for quantum research, distils externally verified workflow repairs into reusable experience. Every reproduced result joins an extensible platform built on the model context protocol, so verified research workflows are directly accessible to AI scientists and new papers can keep being added.
